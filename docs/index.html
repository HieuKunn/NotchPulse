Engineered for macOS

Supercharge Your MacBook Notch

Built natively in Swift with 0% CPU overhead at idle. Experience smooth 120Hz ProMotion animations, biometric conveniences, and smart desktop tools.

Biometric Face ID

Brings iOS-grade facial recognition to your Mac. Continuously senses your presence, previews camera status smoothly, and unlocks protected apps with privacy-first on-device Apple Vision processing.

120Hz Dual Notch / Island

Adapts dynamically whether you are on a built-in MacBook Pro Notch screen or an external Studio Display. Fluidly morphs into a floating Dynamic Island on any monitor at 120Hz ProMotion.

Real-Time Synced Lyrics

Stream live karaoke-style lyrics right across your notch or floating island for Spotify and Apple Music. Hover to scrub tracks, play, pause, or view album artwork in glorious high-DPI.

Notch Shelf & Quick Drop

Turn the physical bezel into an intuitive staging dock. Drag any files, photos, links, or code snippets to the top of your screen to hold them temporarily across workspaces and apps.

External DDC Monitor HUD

Direct hardware DDC/CI control for third-party external monitors. Adjust real hardware brightness, contrast, and audio volume directly with native keyboard shortcut overlays.

Smart Power & MagSafe HUD

Plugging in MagSafe or USB-C triggers a sleek Apple-style battery pulse with real-time wattage speed, estimated time to full charge, and battery health cycle telemetry.
