# NotchPulse v4.8.5

## 🔒 Face ID Enhancements & Cold Wake Reliability
- **First-Try Auto Unlock After Sleep**: Guaranteed smooth, instant face recognition on your very first try when waking your Mac after sleep or idle periods, eliminating dark camera startup frame drops.
- **Ultra-Responsive Lock Screen Retry**: Reduced hover trigger cooldown to 0.5 seconds for faster, seamless re-authentication on the lock screen.
- **Seamless Display Navigation**: Improved Notch display management so if Face ID completes or no face is detected, the Notch gracefully returns to your chosen main display without staying stuck on the camera screen.
- **Zero Battery Strain**: Background AI pipeline pre-warms concurrently on wake while automatically unloading after idle periods to keep memory and battery usage at zero while your Mac sleeps.
