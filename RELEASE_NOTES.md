## NotchPulse v5.5 (Build 454)

### 🛡️ Zero Permission Prompt Spam
- **Complete Elimination of Startup Permission Prompts**: Removed automated upfront permission probing for Camera, Calendar, Reminders, and Apple Events. System authorization dialogs now only appear on-demand when you explicitly configure or interact with that specific feature in Onboarding or Settings.
- **Silent Media Controller Initialization**: Apple Music and Spotify controllers initialize silently without preemptive automation permission challenges.

### 🎵 Real-Time Artwork & Universal Media Synchronization
- **Background Artwork Synchronization**: Album covers and video thumbnails resolve immediately in the background without needing to hover or open the notch.
- **Zero Artwork Cross-Bleed**: Prevents background YouTube tabs from overriding Spotify Web or other media player thumbnails.
- **Instant Adapter Retry**: Ensures Now Playing artwork updates immediately when playback transitions occur.

### 🔒 Face ID Unlock
- **Reliable Password Auto-Fill**: Ensures stored passwords are typed seamlessly and reliably upon face recognition.
