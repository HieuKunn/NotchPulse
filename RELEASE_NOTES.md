# NotchPulse v4.6.2

## 🖱️ Hover Reach & Notch Behavior
- **Adjustable Hover Detection Area**: Added an expanded hover reach slider in Notch behavior settings. You can now open the notch effortlessly by hovering near or below it, without having to reach the exact top edge of your screen.
- **Reliable Open Notch Hovering**: Fixed an issue where the open notch could close prematurely while moving your cursor inside its area. It now stays open steadily until you intentionally move your mouse away.

## 📁 Shelf & Drag Detection
- **Rock-Solid Drag Detection**: Fixed drag-and-drop detection so dragging files, links, or media towards the notch instantly opens the Shelf without getting stuck.
- **Adjustable Drag Reach**: Added a customizable drag hover expansion slider in Shelf settings so the notch opens early when dragging files, preventing conflicts with macOS top-edge window tiling.
- **Refined Border & Visual Polish**: Perfectly rounded dashed drop borders and balanced interior padding across the Shelf.
- **Direct Native AirDrop**: Dropping files onto AirDrop launches the native macOS AirDrop transfer sheet directly.

## 🎛️ System HUDs
- **Persistent HUD Settings**: Fixed an issue where the custom HUD replacement toggle could reset after app restarts or updates. Your preference is now preserved permanently.

## 🖥️ Multi-Display & External Monitors
- **Persistent Display Preference**: Fixed an issue where the notch could automatically reset back to the built-in MacBook screen after waking from sleep or locking your Mac with an external monitor connected.
- **Reliable Reconnection**: Your chosen screen is remembered reliably, ensuring the notch instantly reappears on your preferred display.
