## 🚀 What's New in NotchPulse v5.0 (Build 212)

### 🌊 Ultra-Fluid Apple-Grade Notch Motion
- **Natural Bloom & Collapse**: The Notch now smoothly expands outward and seamlessly collapses inward with authentic Apple-like spring physics, eliminating any visual hitches or abrupt layout jumps.
- **Continuous Width Dynamics**: Seamlessly transitions between compact media states and the fully expanded dashboard without any momentary width drops or snapping.
- **Strict Frame Clipping & Scaling**: Content now scales organically into and out of the camera notch, creating a unified, delightful 120Hz ProMotion experience.

### 🤝 Smart Shake & File Shelf
- **Universal Drag Support**: Easily drop files from macOS Dock stacks, downloads fans, Finder folders, and desktop items directly into the Notch Shelf.
- **Accidental Shake Prevention**: Fine-tuned shake gesture recognition to require intentional, brisk left-and-right shakes, preventing accidental shelf openings while casually moving files across windows.

### 📅 Interactive Calendar & Full Month View
- **Seamless Full Month Expansion**: Click directly on the Month & Year header to instantly open the complete 30-day calendar grid with Lunar dates and schedule details.
- **Optimized Full-Height Display**: Expanded view coverage to comfortably display all dates and upcoming events without cropping.

### 📋 Streamlined Clipboard Manager
- **Instant Non-Blocking Access**: Immediate smooth opening without any pasteboard background latency.
- **Refined Shelf Dimensions**: Optimized clipboard card layout to cleanly fit copied items without empty space.

### ✨ Enhanced Onboarding & Controls
- **Precision Spotlight Highlighting**: The interactive guide now precisely spotlights the Settings gear icon with tailored step positioning.
- **Fluid Automatic Transitions**: Completing or closing the onboarding guide automatically tucks the Notch back into its sleek resting state.
