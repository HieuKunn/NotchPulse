# NotchPulse v4.8.4

## 🔒 Face ID Enhancements & Smart Display Routing
- **Smart Camera Display Routing**: When setting up Face ID or scanning to unlock, the Face ID notch drop-down automatically appears on the display with your active camera (such as your MacBook's built-in screen), ensuring you always look in the right direction.
- **Instant Lock Screen Auto-Unlock**: Restored immediate automatic Face ID scanning on the lock screen for quick, hands-free authentication whenever you wake your Mac.
- **Smoother Recognition & Lighting Warmup**: Improved camera exposure adaptation and facial tracking stability, preventing accidental scan cancellations in changing lighting conditions.
