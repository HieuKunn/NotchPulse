## NotchPulse v5.5 (Build 456)

### 📋 Lossless Full-Quality Clipboard Images
- **Original Quality Image Storage**: Clipboard images are now saved in their 100% original, uncompressed raw format and resolution (PNG, TIFF, WebP, HEIC, JPEG, etc.). Zero downscaling or lossy compression is applied when copied.
- **Flawless Paste Fidelity**: When pasting copied images back into design apps or documents, the exact original aspect ratio, pixel sharpness, and transparent background channels are completely preserved.
- **Crisp High-DPI Notch Previews**: The clipboard list displays clear Retina thumbnails while keeping memory usage near zero.

### 🛡️ Zero Permission Prompt Spam
- **Complete Elimination of Startup Permission Prompts**: Removed all proactive permission probing for Camera, Calendar, Reminders, and Automation at startup. Prompts only trigger on-demand when actively using corresponding features.

### 🎵 Real-Time Artwork & Universal Media Synchronization
- **Background Artwork Synchronization**: Album artwork and video thumbnails resolve immediately in the background without needing to hover or open the notch.
