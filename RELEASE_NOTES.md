## 🌟 Important Release: Liquid Obsidian Glass & Native Bento Cards

> **Important Update:** This milestone release completely overhauls the Notch and Dynamic Island interface into a stunning **Liquid Obsidian Glass** design with Apple-native Bento Cards, precision specular edge lighting, intelligent Mission Control hover protection, and whisper-quiet idle performance.

### 💎 Liquid Obsidian Glass & Specular Edge Lighting
- **Authentic Apple Material Architecture**: Upgraded the entire expanded Notch body to native macOS deep obsidian glass. The upper edge seamlessly swallows the MacBook camera cutout in pure black, while the contour and rounded corners catch and softly refract light from your desktop wallpaper.
- **Cybernetic Specular Rim**: Added a razor-sharp, diamond-chamfered edge highlight along the bottom contour of both the MacBook Notch and Dynamic Island, giving the panel a crisp, physical presence on your screen.
- **Dynamic Adaptability**: Fully respects macOS accessibility settings—when "Reduce Transparency" is turned on, the glass automatically converts to clean, high-contrast opaque surfaces.

### 🍱 Native Bento Cards for Music & Calendar
- **Frosted Bento Layout**: The Music Player and Calendar are now housed in unified, continuous rounded glass cards matching the clean aesthetic of System Settings.
- **Apple Music-Style Controls**: Upgraded the playback controls with a prominent, high-contrast circular Play/Pause button with smooth vector symbol transitions, accent-tinted shuffle/repeat toggles, and refined previous/next buttons.
- **Interactive Scrubber Capsule**: Redesigned the song progress bar into a continuous capsule that smoothly expands when hovered or dragged, featuring a luminous white scrub indicator and clean digital timing labels.
- **Unified Full-Month Expansion**: Expanding your calendar to month view now smoothly transitions across the full card width with continuous spring physics.

### 🛡️ Mission Control Hover Guard
- **Zero Interruption While Multitasking**: Moving your cursor across the top of the screen while in Mission Control or Exposé (window swipe-up view) will no longer trigger the Notch or Dynamic Island to pop open.
- **Instant Resume on Desktop**: Normal hover expansion automatically resumes the instant you select a window or Space and return to your workspace.

### 🎛️ Segmented Glass Tabs & Circular Header Buttons
- **Segmented Capsule Tab Bar**: Modernized the top tab navigation into a sleek floating glass pill bar with smooth matched-geometry sliding indicators and animated icon responses.
- **Refined Action Buttons**: Replaced legacy dark buttons with circular frosted glass buttons for Audio Hub, Display Hub, Camera Preview, and Settings, providing smooth hover highlights.

### ⚡️ 0% Idle CPU & Media Efficiency
- **Completely Paused Idle Timers**: Track progress recalculations and synchronized lyrics evaluation are now explicitly paused whenever the Notch is closed, eliminating all background media rendering cycles and keeping CPU usage at zero when idle.

### 🔒 Polished Lock Screen Media Player
- **Clean Floating Widget**: Streamlined the lock screen music widget with native system glass materials and refined typography, removing unnecessary resize toggles for a rock-solid, distraction-free experience.
