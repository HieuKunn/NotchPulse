## 🌟 Major Release: NotchPulse v5.5

> **Important Update:** This release brings native brightness control for third-party external monitors directly to macOS, introducing the all-new Display Hub right inside your Notch and Dynamic Island with hardware DDC/CI and software dimming support.

### 🖥️ Native Brightness for External Monitors
- **Hardware DDC/CI & Software Dimming Synergy**: Seamlessly control external displays from Dell, LG, Asus, Samsung, BenQ, HKC, and more. Combines hardware backlight adjustment with an instant software window shade fallback to guarantee smooth dimming across all HDMI adapters, USB-C docks, and display types.
- **Save Power & Protect Colors**: Communicates directly with your monitor's display processor to dim the physical backlight whenever possible, maintaining rich contrast and color accuracy.
- **External Monitor Audio Volume**: Easily control your external monitor's built-in speaker volume directly through hardware commands.

### ☀️ All-New Display Hub in the Notch
- **Right-Side Notch Access**: Access all your display controls instantly via the new sun icon situated on the right side of the Notch or Dynamic Island header, sitting cleanly alongside your Audio Hub and Mirror controls.
- **Independent Display Sliders**: View all connected screens (built-in MacBook Retina display and external monitors) at a glance, with dedicated smooth brightness sliders for each display.
- **Scroll-Wheel Fine Tuning**: Simply hover over any brightness slider and scroll with your trackpad or mouse wheel to fine-tune brightness in subtle, precise increments.
- **Quick Preset Buttons**: Jump straight to 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100% brightness with one single click on the quick preset tags.

### 🎨 Refined Layout & Physical Notch Geometry
- **Hardware Notch Alignment**: Inline HUDs and closed media widgets now feature strict physical camera clearance and balanced symmetric wings, preventing icons and text from being obscured by the hardware cutout.
- **Compact & Clean Hub Design**: Display Hub and Audio Hub now feature tailored dynamic heights with compact bottom margins, eliminating unnecessary empty space for a sleek, native look on both Notch and Dynamic Island.
- **Enhanced Dynamic Island HUD Motion**: Default HUD media visualizers and status indicators smoothly transition to the edges when expanding the island.
