# NotchPulse v4.8

## 🔒 Advanced Anti-Spoofing & Fine-Grained Face ID Precision
- **Advanced Screen & Photo Spoof Defense**: Enhanced the live camera recognition engine with landscape and portrait device bezel detection. Face ID now accurately distinguishes between a real person sitting in front of the Mac and a photo or video held up on a phone or tablet screen, preventing unauthorized spoof unlocks.
- **Fine-Grained Match Accuracy Slider**: Upgraded Face ID recognition settings with a 5-step precision slider—ranging from Less Strict, Moderate, Balanced, Strict, to Maximum Security. You can now tune recognition confidence to your exact preference without having to choose between extremes.
- **Intelligent Face Distance & Size Filtering**: Calibrated face size boundaries so that small, distant faces displayed on secondary mobile screens are ignored, ensuring unlock only activates for the user sitting directly before the Mac.
- **Instant Anti-Spoof Guard**: Immediate protection stops scanning as soon as a phone screen or synthetic bezel is spotted, keeping your Mac secure at all times.
- **Smoother & More Responsive Unlocks**: Streamlined per-frame matching so enrolled faces are recognized smoothly and instantly without hesitation upon waking or hovering.
