# NotchPulse v4.8.5

## 📐 Symmetrical Center-Mid HUD Layout & Unified Geometry
- **Perfect Center-Mid HUD Alignment**: Default system controls (volume, brightness, backlight, mic) and standard music popups are now centered both horizontally and vertically with zero edge-clipping or bottom-border crowding.
- **Unified Notch & Dynamic Island UI**: Enforced 100% identical spacing, padding, and width geometry across both Notch and Dynamic Island modes.
- **Spacious Inline Album Art**: Expanded left-side margin for inline media view so album artwork is never cramped against the left corner boundary.
- **Clean Default HUD Modes**: Suppressed top inline media during active bottom-row HUD notifications, ensuring default HUDs and top inline content never render simultaneously.

## 🔒 Face ID Enhancements & Cold Wake Reliability
- **First-Try Auto Unlock After Sleep**: Guaranteed smooth, instant face recognition on your very first try when waking your Mac after sleep or idle periods.
- **Ultra-Responsive Lock Screen Retry**: Reduced hover trigger cooldown to 0.5 seconds for faster, seamless re-authentication on the lock screen.
- **Seamless Display Navigation**: Improved Notch display management so if Face ID completes or no face is detected, the Notch gracefully returns to your chosen main display without staying stuck.
- **Zero Battery Strain**: Background AI pipeline pre-warms concurrently on wake while automatically unloading after idle periods to keep memory and battery usage at zero while your Mac sleeps.
