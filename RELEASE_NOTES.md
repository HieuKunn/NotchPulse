# NotchPulse v3.8.7

## 🔋 Smart Battery & Charging
- **Reliable Charging Limits**: When you set a charge limit (like 80%), NotchPulse now keeps it active reliably in the background without letting the battery accidentally charge to 100%.
- **3 Clear Charging Modes**:
  - **Charge to Limit**: Stops charging automatically at your chosen percentage and powers your Mac directly from the charger.
  - **Charge to 100%**: Charges your battery all the way to full when you need maximum battery life on the go.
  - **Pause Charging**: Pauses charging immediately at your current battery level.

## 🔒 Face ID & Fast Login
- **Arm's Length Detection**: Face ID can now easily recognize you from a comfortable sitting distance (~1 arm's length) without having to lean into the camera.
- **Smarter Auto-Retry**: Automatically retries scanning up to 3 times if your face isn't immediately detected on the first try.
- **Touch ID & Passkey Auto-Fill**: Seamlessly unlocks system prompts, Safari login dialogs, and Passkeys using your face.
- **Smooth Password Fallback**: You can still type your password or use Touch ID on the lock screen anytime with zero delay.

## 🎵 Lock Screen Music & Lyrics
- **Live Karaoke Lyrics**: Lyrics now scroll in real-time smoothly even when the music player is in compact mode.
- **Cleaner Track Transitions**: Added gentle musical notes (`♪ ♫ ♪`) during intro melodies and guitar solos to keep the screen layout smooth.
- **Auto-Fit Screen Size**: The lock screen player automatically adjusts its height when lyrics load so nothing gets cut off.
